I submit for your consideration the enclosed manuscript titled "Skill-Validated Season-Stratified Rainfall Trigger Thresholds for Flash Flood Anticipatory Action in Sylhet Division, Bangladesh" for publication in the International Journal of Disaster Risk Reduction.

This study derives and independently validates the first operational rainfall trigger thresholds for flash flood anticipatory action (AA) in Sylhet Division, Bangladesh, where an estimated 88 percent of flash-flood-affected populations remain outside current AA programming. The analysis integrates 44 years of CHIRPS satellite precipitation, 75 years of ERA5-Land reanalysis over both the local and trans-boundary catchments, seven Sentinel-1 SAR flood events, and 18 documented events sourced from OCHA, IFRC, and peer-reviewed literature.

The paper contributes three findings that we believe will interest IJDRR readers. First, we quantify a season-stratified trigger that exploits the physical distinction between pre-monsoon orographic events (best predicted by upstream Meghalaya rainfall at a 3-day, 19 mm threshold; probability of detection 0.63 with 95 percent confidence interval 0.51 to 0.74) and monsoon events (best predicted by local Sylhet 7-day rainfall at 206 mm; probability of detection 0.35). Season stratification improves overall skill by 34 percent over a single trigger. Second, we demonstrate a benefit-cost ratio near 7:1 based on published anticipatory action unit costs and return-on-investment multipliers, with total avoided damages of approximately USD 1 billion across the seven events, comparable to the 2022 Post-Disaster Needs Assessment total for the June 2022 event. Third, we report an unexpected trend diagnosis: local rainfall totals are stationary over 44 years but consecutive dry days lengthen significantly (0.30 days per year, p = 0.017), indicating that flash flood impact escalation in Sylhet cannot be attributed to increased rainfall totals alone.

All results are reproducible from the code and data archived at GitHub (https://github.com/Naimul-islam-bd/sylhet_flash_flood_aa) and Zenodo.
